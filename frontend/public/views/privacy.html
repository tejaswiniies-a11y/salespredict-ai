Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

SalesPredict AI may collect account information such as name, email, password credentials, user role, prediction inputs, uploaded dataset details, generated forecast results, and report history.

How We Use Information

Collected information is used to create and manage user accounts, authenticate users, generate sales predictions, store prediction history, display dashboard analytics, export reports, and support admin monitoring features.

Uploaded Files and Prediction Data

Uploaded CSV files and prediction inputs may be processed by the machine learning workflow to train or generate forecasts. Users should avoid uploading sensitive personal, financial, or confidential business data unless it is necessary and authorized.

Data Sharing

We do not sell user data. Information may be visible to authorized administrators for system management, user activity review, dataset handling, and platform support.

Data Security

The application uses authentication and protected routes to reduce unauthorized access. However, no software system can guarantee complete security, so users should use strong passwords and protect exported reports carefully.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use essential cookies (specifically JWT tokens) to maintain your session and ensure secure access to your dashboard. These cookies are necessary for the platform to function. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies for marketing purposes.

International Data Transfers

Since SalesPredict AI utilizes cloud-based services like MongoDB Atlas, your data may be stored or processed in countries outside your own. By using our services, you understand and agree that your information may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions.

Data Anonymization for Model Improvement

We may use anonymized and aggregated data—where all personal identifiers have been removed—to improve the accuracy of our machine learning models and enhance the platform's forecasting capabilities. This process helps us identify broad market trends without compromising individual user privacy.

Third-Party Service Providers

We engage trusted third-party service providers to support our operations, including cloud infrastructure providers (such as MongoDB Atlas for data storage) and security services. These partners are permitted to use your information only as necessary to provide these services to us and are obligated to maintain its confidentiality.

Data Retention

Prediction history, uploaded records, and account information may be stored while the account or project remains active. Administrators may remove old or unnecessary data when it is no longer required.

User Rights and Choices

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your personal information and prediction history. You can manage your account details through the dashboard or contact us directly to exercise these rights.

Children's Privacy

SalesPredict AI is intended for professional business use and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we discover such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated when features, storage methods, or data practices change. Users should review this page periodically for the latest information.
